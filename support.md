# Support for "Corsairs: Pirate Adventure" Application

If you have any questions, experience any issues with the game (including in-app purchases or Sign in with Apple), or have suggestions, please contact us.

**Contact Email:** sailandfire@gmail.com

To help us assist you faster, please include the following information in your email:
1. Your device model (e.g., iPhone 14).
2. Your iOS version.
3. A detailed description of the issue (please attach screenshots if applicable).

We will try to respond to you within 24-48 hours.
